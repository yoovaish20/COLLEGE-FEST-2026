ABOUT THE FEST

More Than Just a Fest.

VIBE 26 brings together thousands of students from across the country under one campus-wide celebration. It is not just an event — it is an experience that connects people through what they love.

From intense coding hackathons to electrifying music performances, from competitive sports to creative art exhibitions — VIBE 26 is where every student finds their stage.
